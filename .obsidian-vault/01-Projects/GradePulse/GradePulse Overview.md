# GradePulse Overview

**What:** AI-powered student performance intelligence system for Nigerian schools.
**How:** Uses a LinUCB contextual bandit to recommend the right content to the right student at the right time.
**Signal:** Teachers' weekly test scores — no extra data collection required.
**Team:** 6 AI agents + 1 human founder ([[Divine — Founder]]).

---

## Why It Matters

- Teachers manage 40-80 students with no way to personalize
- Most LMS platforms are content repositories — GradePulse is a **recommendation engine**
- Gets smarter with every score a teacher enters
- Built specifically for the Nigerian education market (WAEC grading, school hierarchy)

---

## What Makes It Different

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Online learning | Improves with every interaction, not just during training |
| Anti-gaming | Students can't exploit by scoring low first |
| Neural Score | 7-dimension self-diagnostic — the brain checks its own health |
| Offline mode | Teachers without internet use a downloadable HTML tool |
| Multi-tenant | School → SchoolClass → Student hierarchy |
