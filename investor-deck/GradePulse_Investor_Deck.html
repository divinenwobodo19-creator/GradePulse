The Problem

Every student gets the same content

In Nigeria, teachers manage 40-80 students per class. Every student receives identical content regardless of ability.

A student who scored 90% last week gets the same exercises as one who scored 15%. There is no personalization, no adaptive learning.

  • No way to identify struggling students early
  • Hours spent manually analyzing test scores
  • Existing LMS platforms are content repositories — they don't learn
  • Teachers can't scale personalized attention
2
The Solution

A recommendation engine that learns what works

GradePulse uses the teacher's existing weekly test scores as its signal — no extra data collection required.

After every score entry, the model updates its understanding and recommends the right content at the right time.
